App Capabilities

Designed for Health, Accessibility, and Peace of Mind

Pillara replaces clinical complexity with warm, encouraging, and easy-to-use helpers.

AI Prescription Scanner

No tedious typing. Simply take a photo of your doctor's handwritten prescription. Our advanced medical AI automatically reads the dosage, timings, and schedules for you.

Smart, Gentle Reminders

Never get startled by clinical alarms. Pillara uses warm soundscapes and customizable verbal alerts that feel like a friendly tap on the shoulder. Support for snooze & flexible timings.

AI Health Assistant

Ask anything about your pills. "Can I take this with milk?" "What side effects should I watch for?" Get immediate, evidence-based, easy-to-understand responses verified by health databases.

Caregiver Sync
